# Executive Summary: Ledgerly Marketing Budget

## Recommendation

Move about 12% of the paid marketing budget (around $2.6M at 2024 levels) from review sites and paid search into accountant partners, webinars and content. Total spend stays the same. Expected result: about $3.5M more in 12-month revenue, with a likely range of $0.5M to $6.5M.

I'd start with a 90-day test before moving the full amount.

## Why

Our dashboard gives all the credit for a new customer to the last click. That hands paid search 56% of the credit. When every touch in the customer's journey counts, paid search earned 28%. Most of its last-click wins are people searching for our brand name after another channel had already done the work.

Some channels return a lot more per dollar than others:

| Channel | Revenue per $1 (first 12 months) | Months to pay back |
|---|---|---|
| Accountant partners | $5.59 | 2.1 |
| Content/SEO | $4.08 | 2.9 |
| Webinars | $3.33 | 3.6 |
| LinkedIn | $3.10 | 3.9 |
| Paid search | $2.27 | 5.3 |
| Review sites | $1.81 | 6.6 |

The better channels bring in bigger customers who stay longer. Partners convert 47% of their leads and keep 84% of them for at least a year. Review sites convert 7% and lose 60% within a year.

## Proposed changes

- Review sites: -30%
- Paid search: -20%, starting with the non-brand "expense cards" campaign, which has the lowest return in the channel
- Accountant partners: +70%
- Webinars: +40%
- Content/SEO: +35%
- LinkedIn: no change

Brand search isn't part of this cut. It also protects our name from competitor bids, so any change there should be tested separately.

## How confident we are

The ranking holds under every way of assigning credit except last-click, and 2025 signups (not used in the main analysis) show the same order. The estimate assumes cuts lose revenue at full rates while new money only earns 75%, so it leans conservative.

## Risks

- These are revenue figures, not profit.
- The partner program may not be able to grow 70% quickly.
- Attribution shows which channels were involved, not which ones caused the sale.

## Next steps

1. Run the shift in about 20% of regions for 90 days and keep the rest unchanged for comparison.
2. Scale up if new spend earns at least 75% of today's average return.
3. Start reporting every-touch credit alongside last-click.
