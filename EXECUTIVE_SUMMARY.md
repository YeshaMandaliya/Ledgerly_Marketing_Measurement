# Executive Summary: Rebalancing Ledgerly's Marketing Budget

**Decision needed:** approve a 90-day test of a budget shift, at no extra spend.

---

## The answer
Move **~12% of the paid marketing budget** (≈ $2.6M at 2024 levels; ≈ $3.3M on the 2025 budget) **out of review sites and paid search** and **into accountant partners, webinars and content**. Total spend stays the same.

**Expected result: +$3.5M in 12-month revenue** (range +$0.5M to +$6.5M).

## Why
**1. Our dashboard gives credit to the wrong channel.**
It credits each customer to the last click before signup, which hands paid search **56%** of the credit. Counting every touch along the way, paid search earned **28%**. Most of its "wins" are people searching our brand name after another channel had already convinced them.

**2. Return per dollar differs 3× across channels.**

| Channel | Revenue per $1 (12 months) | Months to recover acquisition cost |
|---|---|---|
| Accountant partners | **$5.59** | **2.1** |
| Content/SEO | $4.08 | 2.9 |
| Webinars | $3.33 | 3.6 |
| LinkedIn | $3.10 | 3.9 |
| Paid search | $2.27 | 5.3 |
| Review sites | **$1.81** | **6.6** |

**3. The best channels bring bigger customers who stay.**
Partners convert **47%** of leads, and only 16% of those customers leave within a year. Review sites convert **7%**, and **60%** leave. A 51–200-employee customer earns about **19×** a 1–10-employee one, and partners and LinkedIn bring the most of them.

## The plan

| Channel | Change |
|---|---|
| Review sites | −30% |
| Paid search (non-brand "expense cards" campaign first; it returns $1.75 per $1) | −20% |
| Accountant partners | +70% |
| Webinars | +40% |
| Content/SEO | +35% |
| LinkedIn | Hold |

## How sure are we?
- **The ranking holds under every way of assigning credit** except last-click.
- **It holds on new data:** 2025 signups, which the analysis never used, show the identical ranking.
- **The estimate is conservative:** cuts are assumed to lose revenue at full rates, while new money is assumed to earn only 75%. Cutting the weakest paid search campaign first could lift the gain to about +$4.3M.

## Risks
- **Revenue, not profit:** credit and fraud losses aren't measured.
- **Capacity:** partners may not absorb +70% quickly. The test will show this.
- **Brand search** also protects our name from competitor bids, so it isn't cut in this plan; any brand reduction gets its own test.
- **Attribution shows who was present, not who caused the sale.** That's why we test before committing.

## Next steps
1. **Weeks 1–12:** run the shift in ~20% of regions and hold the rest unchanged for comparison.
2. **Week 12 review:** scale up if new money earns at least 75% of today's average return.
3. **Every quarter:** report on every-touch credit alongside last-click.
